FRONT — ANY ESSAY

NAME → SPOT → FORM → CONTEXT → WHY. Name artist/title/medium/date-ish. Find 2 concrete visual details. Describe how they direct the eye or meaning. Connect to religion, patron, politics, market or technology. State significance.

Template: “In [artwork], [formal features] emphasize [message]. The [specific detail] directs attention to [thing]. This reflects [period/region] context of [religion/politics/patronage/technology].”

Compare: same subject/function → body/space/light/material differences → why patron/period changes meaning.

Terms: fresco=paint on plaster; tempera=egg paint; oil=slow-drying detail; engraving=incised printed metal; relief=raised design; contrapposto=shifted weight; foreshortening=depth compression; linear perspective=orthogonals→vanishing point; atmospheric perspective=fading distance; chiaroscuro=light/shadow modeling; tenebrism=spotlight vs near-black; sfumato=smoky edge; colorito=color; disegno=design; iconography=subject symbolism; genre=daily life; vanitas=mortality.

BACK — HISTORICAL ANCHORS

Italy 1300: Catholic devotional images, mendicant church patronage; Giotto=weight, emotional narrative; Byzantine tradition=gold/icon.

Flanders 1400: merchants, private devotion; oil glazes, hidden symbols, detailed interiors.

Florence 1400: humanism, classical revival, Medici/civic prestige; Brunelleschi perspective/dome; Masaccio depth.

Italy 1500: papal commissions, ideal anatomy, balanced composition; Venice=color; Mannerism=distortion/virtuosity.

Reformation 1517+: Luther, printing, distinct Catholic/Protestant art settings; Council of Trent 1545–63.

Baroque Italy/Spain: emotional motion/light, Catholic spectacle, court commissions. Dutch: trade/middle-class home art, still life/genre, global exploitation. France: Louis XIV/Versailles=absolute monarchy/classical control.

China: Yuan ink scroll/literati; cobalt porcelain. Indigenous Americas: Mexica temple ritual; Inka terraces; Pueblo black-on-black; Kwakwaka'wakw performance masks; ledger histories. Mughal India: emperor/holy man, symmetrical tomb gardens; Madurai: Hindu Nayak gopura.

David shortcut: Donatello WON; Michelangelo WAITS; Bernini THROWS.
