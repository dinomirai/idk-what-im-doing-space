   explained in html.html and structure.html.

   explained in structure.html.
3. Ordered and unordered lists: learning path and examples in structure.html.

7. Images with alt attributes: useful descriptions on the house image;
   empty alt on the decorative browser icon; explained in accessibility.html.
8. Repeated classes: box, note, checkpoint, demo-box, mini-card and others.
9. IDs for structural sections: lesson, section headings and checkpoint sections.
10. Box model: margin, padding and borders; explanation and demo in layout.html.
11. Float, clear and clearfix: sidebar and footer plus the floated house demo;
    layout.html explains the ::after clearfix with its full CSS.
12. Flexbox: wrapping card-row demo in layout.html and the lesson pager in CSS.
13. Margin-auto centering: ie-window; explanation and .page example in layout.html.
14. Background styling: desktop, frame, lesson, result and note backgrounds.
15. RGBA colour: note background; explained in colours.html.
16. HTML and CSS comments: explain browser decoration, shared directory,
    design system, keyboard focus, layout and clearfix; structure.html teaches them.
17. Appropriate image format and size: local 96x64 PNG drawing and 32x32 PNG
    icon; links.html explains PNG versus JPEG and appropriate image dimensions.
    No claim is made that Photoshop or Figma was used.

RUBRIC MAP
Structure of Learning: explicit beginner prerequisites; numbered learning path;
  increasingly complex lessons; a cumulative practice task.
Learning Format: short written explanations, selectable code, visible results,
  images and self-check feedback; no video or sound required.
Navigation and Organization: consistent directory, current-page marker,
  previous/next links, skip link, section links, glossary and FAQ.
Clarity of Explanation: objectives, small examples, definitions, common
  mistakes, expected results and practice questions.
Friction and Frustration: offline use, no account or installation, manageable
  sections, readable code boxes and a responsive layout.
Accessibility: text alternatives, readable typography and colour pairs,
  labelled input, visible keyboard focus and meaningful headings/link labels.
