Your learning path

Start with a page of text, add styles, connect pages, then check your finished work. Each lesson includes an objective, code, a visible example, practice and an answer you can reveal.

  1. Basic HTML
    Create your first page · about 6 minutes
  2. Headings & lists
    Organise your content · about 5 minutes
  3. Basic CSS
    Connect one stylesheet · about 5 minutes
  4. Colours & fonts
    Make your text readable · about 4 minutes
  5. Links & images
    Connect pages and pictures · about 6 minutes
  6. Boxes & layout
    Arrange and space content · about 7 minutes
  7. Small screens
    Make a page fit its window · about 5 minutes
  8. Accessibility
    Check keyboard use and labels · about 6 minutes
  9. Click-open answers
    Learn the researched technique · about 4 minutes
  10. Put it together
    Build a small practice site · about 10 minutes

Set up a practice folder

  1. Use any plain text editor. No extensions or special software are needed.
  2. Create a folder named my-practice-site.
  3. Save your page as index.html. If your editor asks, choose plain text or “All files” so it does not add .txt.
  4. Open index.html in your browser. Save your edits, then refresh to see them.
my-practice-site/
    index.html
    style.css       (created in lesson 3)
    images/         (used in lesson 5)

Keep your practice folder separate from this tutorial so you can refer to the lessons while you work. You will build a two-page practice site; this tutorial itself has more than four pages.

Learn at your own pace

Allow about an hour for the full path, or take one lesson at a time. At the end, you should be able to structure a page, share a stylesheet, add links and images, arrange content and check basic accessibility.

The numbered menu shows your current page. Use the previous and next links to follow the path. If you forget a word, use the glossary. For a problem, use the FAQ.

Everything runs locally. Lessons, pictures and examples work offline. Only the optional external reference links need internet access.
